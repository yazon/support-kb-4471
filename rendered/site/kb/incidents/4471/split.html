parser: the shared parser reads month first

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so day and month come out swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” It reaches people through the app, by email and through booking-v2’s form.

Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales, with #4471 closed on 5 October. Ticket closed as completed.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first

Same symptom, different code. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at a day-first tenant type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it day first, the tenant’s way. Only reports through booking-v3’s form, from the tenants that run it.

Won’t fix: booking-v3’s form is retired. The parser fix does not touch this form, so these reports end with the form, not with a fix. Ticket closed as not planned.

one-day: dates move by one day

A swap of day and month cannot move a date by one day, and these reports describe exactly that:

  • “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.”
  • “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.”
  • “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.”

The desk read them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). CauseTable files them under parser by tenant, with a footnote; nothing else ever came of the footnote. The Lisbon and Toronto report points at a time zone: a date turned into an instant and back in the wrong zone.

Open: neither the parser fix nor retiring booking-v3 moves a date back by one day. Investigate the time-zone handling of invite dates; the ticket stays open until then.
